**证明：**  
首先，引入函数 \( g: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R} \) 定义为：  
\[
g(x) = \left( \frac{4}{\pi} \right)^{n/4} e^{-2 |x|^2}.
\]  
从而有
\[
\int_{\mathbb{R}^n} g(x-y) g(x-z)  dx = \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} \left( \frac{4}{\pi} \right)^{n/2} e^{-2 |x-y|^2} e^{-2 |x-z|^2}  dx.
\]  
简化指数：  
\[
|x-y|^2 + |x-z|^2 = 2 \left| x - \frac{y+z}{2} \right|^2 + \frac{1}{2} |y-z|^2.
\]  
因此，  
\[
\int_{\mathbb{R}^n} g(x-y) g(x-z)  dx = \left( \frac{4}{\pi} \right)^{n/2} e^{- |y-z|^2} \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} e^{-4 \left| x - \frac{y+z}{2} \right|^2}  dx.
\]  
所以  
\[
\int_{\mathbb{R}^n} g(x-y) g(x-z)  dx = e^{-|y-z|^2}.
\]  
定义卷积 \( h(x) = (f * g)(x) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} f(y) k(x-y)  dy \)。  
\[
\int_{\mathbb{R}^n} |h(x)|^2  dx = \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} \left| \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} f(y) g(x-y)  dy \right|^2  dx.
\]  
展开有 
\[
\int_{\mathbb{R}^n} |h(x)|^2  dx = \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} f(y) f(z) \left( \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} g(x-y) g(x-z)  dx \right)  dy  dz = \int_{\mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}^n} f(y) f(z) e^{-|y-z|^2}  dy  dz.
\]  
由于 \( \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} |h(x)|^2  dx \geq 0 \)，故原积分非负。
